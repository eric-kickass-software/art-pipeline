Layers

Drag: shoulder/hip, elbow/knee, wrist/ankle, fingertip/toe, neck/pelvis/crotch, waistband, face guides (front head: eyes, mouth, centre line). Click a part (or its layer) to select it in both views, then drag its outline points (double-click the outline to add one, Alt-click a point to remove it). Limb points belong to their bone: moving a joint carries the outline along (the round ends around the joints are the overlap under the parent). Zoom: mouse wheel (up to 8×); pan: right- or middle-drag, or hold Space and drag. Ctrl+Z undoes. Hidden layers can't be clicked.

A skin tone (or any colour) is a ramp of colours, darkest first. The art keeps its cel levels and the game colours them from the chosen ramp, so one painted puppet serves every colour. The preview shows the race's painted male and female puppets (approved Paint, else the newest) wearing its hair and face (Face tab: first in the set, else the newest); the ramp you edit colours its own layer (skin, hair or eyes). A race's colour set is every skin, hair and eye ramp tagged with it. Saved to specs/palettes.json.
