Layers

Drag: shoulder/hip, elbow/knee, wrist/ankle, fingertip/toe, neck/pelvis/crotch, waistband. Click a part (or its layer) to select it in both views. A limb segment is one path: a circle at each joint joined by straight sides; drag a circle's square to resize it (an upper arm/thigh's first circle is the shoulder/hip circle). Torso and head: outline points (double-click the outline to add one, Alt-click a point to remove it). Zoom: mouse wheel (up to 8×); pan: right- or middle-drag, or hold Space and drag. Ctrl+Z undoes. Hidden layers can't be clicked.
